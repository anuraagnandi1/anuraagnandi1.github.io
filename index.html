Pakka A concept for Scapia
Screen 1 of 6

Every “yes” in a group chat is secretly an “if”.

Friend trips die in a deadlock of hidden conditions. Pakka lets each friend say “I’m in, if…”, holds their share without charging it, and books the trip the moment every “if” is true at the same time.

Plan
A two-night Goa trip with college friends who now live in different cities.
User
The organiser, who usually pays the advance and then chases everyone for it.

Things to try. On screen 4, change Karan’s “if”. On screen 5, make Rahul drop out, then save the trip. On screen 2, paste your own group chat.

Concept prototype by Nandi Anuraag Vishwanath for the Scapia product internship assignment. Not an official Scapia product. The people, chat, prices and payments are made up for this demo, and no money moves anywhere.
